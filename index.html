03 — Intelligence Layer

Dual-Model Architecture

Type Mind Spark uses two AI engines for different types of tasks—one optimized for speed, the other for deeper reasoning.

🟣 AURA EngineOptimized for low-latency responses

AURA Engine

Built for fast, everyday writing. AURA handles quick suggestions, rewrites and tone adjustments without interrupting your typing flow.

  • Fast messaging & social media replies
  • Grammar, punctuation, and flow polish
  • Available across Free, Plus, Pro, Ultra & Enterprise tiers
Powered by lightweight LLM APIsDefault Pipeline
🔵 PRISM EngineDeep Reasoning & Synthesis

PRISM Engine

Built for complex writing and deeper reasoning. PRISM handles longer prompts, strategic writing, research and tasks where more reasoning is useful.

  • Advanced goal-based persuasion & negotiation
  • Deep historical memory integration & tone mimicry
  • Exclusive to Pro, Ultra & Enterprise tiers
Advanced reasoning engineDeep Reasoning
